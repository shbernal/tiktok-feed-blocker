Block TikTok's most distracting surfaces and take control of your time. TikTok Feed Blocker lets you block the Home, Explore, and Live pages individually while keeping the rest of TikTok stable. It's built for people who want intentional access to TikTok without getting pulled into endless scrolling.

Features:

- Blocks TikTok Home, Explore, and Live pages
- Turn blocking on or off for each page separately
- One-click popup controls, plus a quick shortcut to toggle the current page
- In-page controls make it easy to re-block a page without opening the popup
- Mutes videos and audio in blocked sections
- Lightweight, fast, and simple to use

Your settings are stored locally in the browser. The extension collects nothing, sends nothing, and only runs on tiktok.com.

Regain focus and use TikTok on your terms.
